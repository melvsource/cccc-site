How to add a post (recommended structure)

Create a new article in one of these folders: /messages/
/reports/
/updates/
/testimonies/ Then add one card to public/updates.html that links to that article. Change data-cat to message, report, testimony, or update. Fill in the title, date, and excerpt. Replace the img-ph block with a real <img> tag when you have a photo. Save and push to GitHub.
